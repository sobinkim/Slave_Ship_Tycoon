## 요격함 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one standalone transparent-background 2D ship design concept for an existing mobile naval auto-battler.
Input images 1 and 2 are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: match their bold dark burgundy outlines, warm brown wooden hulls, cream sails, small red mast pennant, gold trim, purple-gray iron, chunky readable cartoon proportions and softly shaded solid color shapes. Do not replicate their harpoon or armored defender silhouettes. The art is smooth bold cartoon sprite art, NOT pixel art, NOT a realistic painting.
Primary request: design a new INTERCEPTOR / ANTI-PROJECTILE ESCORT SHIP (요격함). Exactly ONE complete ship.
Subject: a light, nimble narrow wooden cutter with a pointed gold-trimmed prow, low slim hull, one compact cream sail bearing a simple black projectile-interception crosshair emblem, small red pennant. Its unmistakable main equipment is a compact mechanical pivot turret carrying TWO slender parallel rapid-fire flak barrels aiming diagonally up and to the right, with exposed brass crank and restrained small ammunition feed. The double barrels must read instantly at thumbnail size, never a giant single broadside cannon. A small round optical sight beside the turret, no modern radar or electronics. Keep the sail behind the turret and the weapons unobstructed. Light reinforcement plates only; it must look much lighter and faster than the armored reference.
Composition: isolated whole ship, right-facing side view with a small amount of top deck visible, same viewing angle as the references. Centered with generous transparent margins, entirely inside the canvas, consistent outline thickness, clean separated shapes, ready to evaluate as a game sprite. Ship itself fills about 80 percent of the canvas.
Constraints: transparent background with genuine alpha. No ocean, no wake, no ground shadow, no frame, no text labels, no UI, no firing effects, no extra ships, no extra alternate views. Preserve the existing game's nautical fantasy visual language; no modern warship, no sci-fi, no photorealism. This is a concept design, not a change to an existing sprite.

## 박격포함 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one standalone transparent-background 2D ship design concept for the same mobile naval auto-battler as the references.
Input images 1 and 2 are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: match their thick dark burgundy outlines, warm wooden hulls, cream cloth sails, red pennant, gold trim, purple-gray iron, chunky clean cartoon shapes and softly shaded solid fills. Do not copy their main weapons or silhouette. Smooth bold cartoon sprite illustration, NOT pixel art.
Primary request: design one new MORTAR SHIP / BOMB KETCH (박격포함). Exactly ONE ship, complete isolated cutout.
Subject: a short, broad, weighty wooden bomb vessel with a low reinforced hull, squared heavy stern on the left and blunt gold-trimmed bow on the right. Its defining feature is ONE enormous SHORT FAT mortar barrel on the open forward deck, tilted steeply upward about 65 degrees toward the upper right. The upward-facing muzzle is a broad visible dark ellipse, with thick purple-gray iron walls and gold reinforcing bands. Clearly a stubby lob-shot mortar, never a long horizontal battleship cannon or multiple thin flak barrels. The mortar sits on a chunky timber-and-iron recoil cradle with visible braces tied into the hull. A restrained rack of three large round black mortar bombs with brass caps behind it, safely housed on deck. One short aft mast with a compact cream sail showing a simple black mortar-bomb emblem, and a small red pennant. Keep the barrel visually in front of the sail and unobscured. Reinforced side ribs and only a few large riveted plates; broad load-bearing stance, heavy siege role readable at thumbnail scale. Use the same faction colors as the references. No need to depict an attack.
Composition: same right-facing side view with a little top deck visible as the two reference ships, centered whole silhouette with generous transparent margins, ship fills around 80 percent of canvas, nothing clipped, controlled detail and clean negative spaces.
Constraints: genuine fully transparent alpha background; absolutely no colored background glow, no scenery, no ocean, no wake, no cast ground shadow, no border, no text labels, no UI, no muzzle flash, no extra ships or views. Do not draw a modern warship, tank, sci-fi vehicle or photorealistic boat. This is a fresh ship concept in the existing game's art style.
