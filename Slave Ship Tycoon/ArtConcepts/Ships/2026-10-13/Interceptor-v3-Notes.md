Use case: precise-object-edit.
Revise this single-barrel interceptor ship concept. The previous simplification now looks cheap and flimsy; give it convincing functional construction and a stronger silhouette without returning to the overly decorated first design.
Keep exactly ONE long slender cannon barrel, pointing up-right, one cream sail with black crosshair emblem, a small red pennant, the wooden hull, the right-facing side/deck viewpoint, thick dark burgundy outlines and existing bold cartoon game-art style.
Changes: replace the tiny gun perched on a bare wooden block with a substantial compact rotating iron turret cradle: a low rounded drum base integrated into the deck, two sturdy side cheeks around the pivot, a simple recoil sleeve at the barrel root, and a small angular gun shield. The entire gun mount should have about twice the visual mass of the current mount, looking engineered and stable. The barrel should be a little more solid near its base but remain recognizably a quick interceptor cannon, never a fat mortar or giant battleship gun.
Give the bow one purposeful purple-gray riveted armor plate that follows its pointed shape. Add one or two restrained iron reinforcement plates beneath the turret on the hull, and a short raised stern rail; retain ample visible wood. Use only a few large readable bolts. Gold/brass only on two or three functional collars or the pivot center, plus the existing modest mast bindings: no continuous gold racing stripe, no giant golden lightning prow, no filigree. Improve visual balance with a slightly lower, broader gun platform integrated with the hull instead of a wooden pedestal.
Do NOT add a second barrel, glass telescope, ammunition belt, exposed cartridges, crank wheel, extra sails or decorative gadgets. Aim for a believable specialized escort: stronger and better built than a basic boat, but at the same restrained detail level as the existing game's other escort ships.
One complete isolated ship, centered, comfortable margins, no cropped parts. Preserve transparency, genuine alpha background. Remove background glow completely. No ocean, wake, cast shadow, scenery, text or UI.
